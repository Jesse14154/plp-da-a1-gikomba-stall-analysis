## Project Overview

This project analyses 14 days of sales and stock records for Wanjiku's fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market. The purpose is to understand the stall's sales performance, compare results between the two weeks, identify strong and weak sales days, compare weekday and weekend performance, identify products that need restocking, and calculate gross profit margins. The analysis uses core Python to turn the supplied business data into findings and practical recommendations that can support Wanjiku's business decisions.

## Top Three Findings

1. **Total sales:** The stall recorded total sales of **KES 46,450** over the 14-day period.
2. **Weekly growth:** Sales increased from **KES 22,050 in Week 1** to **KES 24,400 in Week 2**, representing growth of approximately **10.66%**.
3. **Weekend performance:** Average weekend sales were **KES 4,900**, approximately **82.50% higher** than the weekday average of **KES 2,685**.
